About Bumi Tunggal

A Service Partner Built Around Reliability, Quality and People.

07 June 2012Date of incorporation

The company was established primarily to supply foreign workers to qualified businesses in Malaysia, and has since developed capabilities in the service sector.

Its immediate geographical focus is the main commercial hubs in Peninsular Malaysia, with revenue streams developed through foreign manpower outsourcing, manpower recruitment and professional services.
